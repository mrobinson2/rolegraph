What this comparison covers

Assignments to users, groups, service principals and managed identities are checked by identity, role and originating scope. Removed or changed conditions are reported. Inherited assignments are reviewed at the scope where they were granted.

Group membership changes, PIM eligibility and deny assignments are outside this comparison. Changes to role permissions are checked for roles pinned in the approved files. This is an assignment review, not a calculation of effective actions.
